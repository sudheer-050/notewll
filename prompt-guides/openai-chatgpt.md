Write the prompt the way OpenAI recommends prompting ChatGPT and GPT models:

- Put the instruction at the start of the prompt.
- Be specific and descriptive about the desired context, outcome, length, format and style. Replace vague words ("short", "good", "a few") with concrete ones ("3 to 5 sentences", "a numbered list of 5 items").
- Separate instructions from any supplied text, code or data with clear delimiters such as triple quotes or a heading like "Text:".
- Show the desired output format when it matters, for example a sample layout or the field names to return.
- Say what to do instead of only what to avoid.
- For reasoning-heavy tasks, keep the prompt simple and direct and state the end goal and what a successful answer must include; do not script every step.
- Mention the audience and the role when they change the answer (for example "explain for a non-technical manager").
- Keep it free of filler and politeness padding.
